# 157 — v0.2 in four milestones: lines and tags, combining, conflicts, `who`

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-11-14
- **Agenda topic:** none (v0.2 planning)
- **Decided by:** Claude, at the owner's request while the owner was away. The owner can reverse any part.
- **Refines:** [decision 001](001-first-audience-and-v0.1.md) (v0.2 is lines and combining); [decision 012](012-lines.md) (the full design of combining comes before v0.2); [decision 018](018-milestone-plan.md) (the milestone plan); [decision 021](021-v0.1-command-scope.md) (`who` in v0.2); [decision 128](128-v0.1-ships-without-goto-within-twice-gits-time.md) (going back within twice Git's time is a goal for v0.2)

## Context

v0.1 is built, and its draft release waits for the owner to publish it. v0.2 comes next. What the decisions already give it:

- lines and combining (Git: branches and merging), with conflicts (decisions 001, 007 and 012);
- renaming and deleting lines (decision 019), and the commands `line new`, `line rename`, `line delete`, `combine`, `resolve` and `tag` (decision 017);
- `who` (decision 021);
- move notes: a save has had room for them since milestone 1, but no save of v0.1 records one (milestone 6 left them to v0.2);
- going back (`goto`) within twice Git's time, as a goal (decision 128).

Decision 012 gave combining a direction only, and said its full design comes before v0.2. That is too much for one milestone. Left to settle: the order, what each milestone leaves working, what v0.2 leaves out, and where the work lives while v0.1 isn't published yet.

## Options

- **A. Combining first, the small line commands after.** The headline arrives early, but combining is of little use until conflicts work too, so nothing can be used until two big pieces are done.
- **B. From the ground up, each milestone usable on its own:** lines and tags, then combining where nothing conflicts, then conflicts, then `who` and the release.
- **C. One milestone.** One plan and one red-team session, but too big to review, and a late finding would unsettle everything built before it.

## Decision

**B: milestones 7 to 10.**

| # | Milestone | Done when |
|---|---|---|
| 7 | **Lines and tags** | `line new`, `line rename` and `line delete` work, `save --line` works from a line, `tag` makes, lists, moves and deletes tags, and a tag's name is taken by `goto` and `show`; Opalro can tell whether one save is in another's history through every parent ([decision 160](160-whether-a-save-is-in-anothers-history.md)) |
| 8 | **Combine** | `combine <line>` joins two lines when no file conflicts: it finds where they parted, merges the folders file by file and text line by line, follows files that moved, and only moves the line forward when that is all it takes. `save` records the moves it is sure of. `history`, `show` and `goto` reach the saves a combine brought in. A combine that would leave a conflict stops, names the files and changes nothing |
| 9 | **Conflicts** | A combine with conflicts is kept: the conflict record is final in the storage spec, text files get markers, other files keep your version, `resolve` records a fix, and every command knows what a conflict is |
| 10 | **`who`, and v0.2** | `who <file>` names the save behind each line of a file; `save --amend` refuses a save another line reaches through a combine; going back is measured against twice Git's time; v0.2 is packaged up to, but not including, publishing |

- **Each milestone's own questions are decided before its plan is written,** as milestones 2 to 6 did it. Decisions [158](158-making-renaming-and-deleting-lines.md) to [160](160-whether-a-save-is-in-anothers-history.md) are milestone 7's.
- **Every milestone ends with its red-team session** (decision 024).
- **Where the work lives.** On the branch `v0.2`. `main` stays what v0.1 is built from until the owner publishes v0.1, so a fix to v0.1 can still be released from it. Each milestone is built on a branch of its own and merged into `v0.2` after its red-team session. `v0.2` goes into `main` only by the owner's hand.
- **Format 1 stays.** Lines, tags and combined saves are all things format 1 already holds: a save has had room for several parents since milestone 1, and a Git import fills them. Only conflicts need something new on disk, and milestone 9 decides how that arrives. Until then, a project used with v0.2 can still be opened by v0.1.
- **`opalro_json` stays 1:** v0.2 adds documents and fields and changes none (decision 117).

**Left out of v0.2,** each in design.md's deferred list:

- **Moving saves between lines, and editing older saves** (Git's rebase and cherry-pick; Jujutsu's rebase and squash). They rebuild a save on top of another one, which makes conflicts of its own. That is what the conflict algebra planned on top of decision 007 is for, so the two come together, after v0.2. `save --amend` stays the one edit.
- **Guessing that a changed file is also a moved one** (Git guesses by how alike two files are). v0.2 follows only the moves it is sure of.
- **Merge programs** (a three-way merge tool that Opalro starts). Starting another program is what decision 142 rules out.
- **Merging by a file's grammar,** and smart merges for game-engine files.
- **A combined save where moving the line forward would do** (Git's `--no-ff`).
- **`reverse`** (v0.3, decision 021).

## Consequences

- [milestones.md](../milestones.md) gains v0.2's table and each milestone's steps as it is planned; design.md's roadmap and deferred list follow.
- Milestone 8 is the largest: it may be planned as two halves, as milestone 6 was.
- The owner comes back to several milestones' worth of calls. Each milestone's rulings file starts with a short please-confirm list, as milestone 6's did.
